The game

Everything a ruler needs

  • 21 kingdoms, 10 ancient cultures

    Take the throne of one of twenty-one kingdoms, from Rome-like Aeteren and Carthage-like Qart-Hadas to the Goths of Gautagard.

  • Battles, sieges and sea battles

    Command your troops in real time. Storm city walls, starve out a fortress or fight it out ship against ship.

  • Diplomacy, plots and succession

    Forge alliances, make enemies and uncover plots at court. When the crown passes on, the succession can change everything.

  • Generals with character

    Every general has tactics, courage, cunning and charisma. Who you put in command shapes every battle.

  • Same rules for everyone

    Rival kingdoms play by exactly the same rules as you. No hidden bonuses, no shortcuts: every victory earned.

  • Fully offline

    No connection needed. Play on the train, on a plane or on the beach.

  • 13 languages

    Play in your own language, with thirteen to choose from.

  • One optional purchase

    Royal Edition removes ads and unlocks extras at once; free players can earn the same extras by watching ads.

Ten cultures

Choose your people

Each culture fights, trades and rules in its own way, inspired by the peoples of the ancient world. Here they are as they look in battle.

  • Aeteren

    Inspired by Rome

    Disciplined legions, straight roads and stone walls built to outlast any king.

  • Qart-Hadas

    Inspired by Carthage

    Merchant princes, war elephants and a fleet that rules the sea lanes.

  • Thalassokartia

    Inspired by Greece

    Phalanxes of citizen soldiers, island harbours and fierce pride in their freedom.

  • Sarissapolis

    Inspired by Macedon

    Long pikes and companion cavalry that once carried a king to the edge of the world.

  • Molossis

    Inspired by Epirus

    Mountain kings with war elephants and a taste for costly victories.

  • Savarania

    Inspired by Persia

    A king of kings with a guard that never shrinks and archers that darken the sky.

  • Karakorum

    Inspired by the Mongols

    Horsemen of the endless steppe who strike before the enemy has formed its lines.

  • Chang’an

    Inspired by China

    Crossbow ranks, iron discipline and a court of a thousand ministers.

  • Vidugavua

    Inspired by the Goths

    Hard warriors from the cold north, loyal to the chief who leads from the front.

  • Dar al-Khilafa

    Inspired by the Caliphate

    Swift desert cavalry and great trading cities on the caravan roads.
